## Result

- **162 verdicts improved, 0 got worse.**
- **0 changes outside the impact set** (a change outside the set would mean the impact analysis missed a coupling).
- In the real editor, 42 of 42 inspector readings match the harness (calibration runs 3 and 4; see `E1.md`).
